## Anti-bloat decisions

- Eleven example prompts were consolidated into nine definitive questions where
  two prompts sought the same complete answer.
- The existing shared pricing answer now holds the full cross-service factor
  list; service pages explain only their own variables.
- Existing travel, delivery/setup, and retainer answers were retained rather
  than duplicated.
- Four new service-level FAQs were added. No pricing route, package table,
  calculator, or article was created.
- Every answer appears before the page's inquiry action.

## Facts published

- Coffee and dessert quotes depend on the selected experience, guest count,
  duration, location, travel, staffing, menu, equipment, setup, conditions,
  branding, multi-day needs, and service-station count as applicable.
- Rental pricing depends on item, quantity, date, location, rental period,
  availability, access, logistics, labour, and operating conditions.
- Rental delivery and setup are not universally included.
- Travel fees may apply outside the standard service area.
- Branding can affect price when it introduces production work.
- Combining services may alter shared logistics but does not create an automatic
  discount or surcharge.
- A signed contract and 30% non-refundable retainer secure the booking; the
  remaining balance is due seven days before the event.

## Withheld dependencies

- Public rates, starting prices, and price ranges.
- Pricing formulas or guest-count and duration weights.
- Per-item rental prices and a universal rental logistics policy.
- Distance thresholds and travel-fee calculations.
- A universal branding fee or production-price schedule.

These remain content dependencies rather than estimated figures.
