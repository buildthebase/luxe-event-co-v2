## Format Differentiation

### Luxe Café Cart Experience

- An intimate, focused café setup.
- Suited to gatherings where warm hospitality should sit naturally within the room.
- Presented as a distinct event-fit choice, not an inferior package.

### Luxe Signature Coffee Bar Experience

- A premium, full-service bar expression with a stronger visual presence.
- Suited to weddings, corporate events, activations, and larger-format celebrations.
- Presented as a distinct event-fit choice, not an upsell label.

Both formats retain the confirmed Luxe coffee inclusions. Guest count, service duration, setting, footprint, staffing, menu, and venue logistics determine the appropriate format.

## Verified Operational Facts

- Confirmed capacity: up to 500 guests.
- Confirmed simultaneous setups: up to three.
- Coffee events generally begin around 30 guests.
- Setup and takedown are included.
- Hot and iced beverages are included.
- Four espresso classics are included.
- Two signature coffee drinks are included.
- One premium non-coffee beverage is included.
- Dairy and premium milk alternatives are included.
- Service is available across Toronto and the GTA, with select Southern Ontario destination events considered subject to applicable travel fees.
- Luxe Event Co. carries $5 million in liability insurance.

Capacity is not presented as a fixed drinks-per-hour promise. Staffing, menu complexity, service duration, venue access, and guest flow remain event-specific.

## Deferred Operational Boundary

The intake does not yet confirm one universal footprint, electrical load, water configuration, barista count, or drinks-per-hour rate for every coffee format.

The page therefore states that:

- Space, power, and water requirements depend on the selected format, menu, venue, and indoor or outdoor setting.
- Luxe confirms exact venue requirements during planning.
- No universal measurement or self-contained utility claim should be assumed.

Final production specifications should replace this boundary only after client approval.
