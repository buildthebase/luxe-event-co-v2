## Decision

units. Their natural-language variants inherit the canonical cluster's
classification. This avoids treating equivalent wording as separate content
requirements.

No new route or public content block is approved by this step. Every current
answer belongs first on an existing conversion page. Future-resource ideas
remain conditional on evidence and do not form a content calendar.

## Answer-status interpretation

- **Answered** means a useful, qualified answer already exists. It does not
  mean Luxe has approved every possible number or operating detail.
- **Partially answered** means the page covers the decision but lacks evidence
  needed for a more specific or complete answer.
- **Dependency blocked** means the exact answer cannot be published yet. A
  page may still explain what Luxe reviews without estimating the missing fact.

## Future-resource gate

The strongest eventual resources are permissioned case studies, a verified
venue-requirements sheet, an approved brand-production guide, and a maintained
rental inventory. None is recommended for implementation now. Each depends on
facts, media, operational ownership, or update discipline that is not yet
available.

City pages remain excluded. Geographic intent is satisfied by the approved
Toronto/GTA service-area hierarchy and natural local context unless Luxe later
has sufficient unique evidence for a genuinely useful local page.

## Source-of-truth issue resolved in Step 4B.9

The Corporate Events and Brand Activations pages previously stated that Luxe
supports multi-day and recurring work while the dependency register marked
those operating models unverified. Step 4B.9 removed the promises. The pages now
state that such requests may be reviewed but cannot be represented as available
until dates, staffing, equipment, resets, storage, replenishment, access,
locations, and travel are confirmed. The up-to-three simultaneous Coffee Bar
and Sweet Cart limits remain published because that capability is confirmed.

## Implementation rules

- Keep one complete answer on the narrowest responsible existing page.
- Use contextual links instead of duplicating full answers.
- Do not estimate missing operational facts.
- Do not convert every question into an FAQ.
- Do not add FAQ schema unless the visible FAQ exactly contains the answer.
- Do not interpret future-resource potential as permission to create a route.
