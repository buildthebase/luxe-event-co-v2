## Experience Differentiation

Luxe Sweet Cart is defined by:

- Desserts prepared live and on-site.
- A cart selected as part of the event styling rather than treated as a generic serving station.
- Guest interaction through made-to-order preparation and finishing.
- A tactile menu of sauces, toppings, and enhancements.
- Professional attendants and a coordinated presentation.

This is distinct from Luxe Coffee Bar's crafted beverage hospitality and Luxe Seating Rentals' architectural role in shaping the room. The page connects those divisions only where they create a credible combined event experience.

### Classic Collection

- Sleek, timeless, and versatile.
- Designed to sit quietly within the event styling.
- Presented as a visual-character choice, not an inferior package.

### Signature Collection

- Bold, elegant, and a statement piece.
- Intended to hold a stronger decorative presence in the room.
- Presented as a visual-character choice, not an automatic upgrade.

Cart availability, dessert service, styling, footprint, and venue access determine the appropriate collection.

## Verified Operational Facts

- Confirmed capacity: up to 400 guests.
- Confirmed simultaneous setups: up to three.
- Desserts include Mini Dutch Pancakes, Belgian Waffles on a Stick, and Mini Donuts.
- Soft Serve Ice Cream is available as an optional enhancement.
- Core desserts are freshly prepared on-site.
- Premium sauces and a selection of standard toppings are included.
- Premium toppings are available as enhancements.
- Professional attendants, serving supplies, an elegant menu display, setup, and takedown are included.
- Custom signage and branding can be considered.
- Service is available across Toronto and the GTA, with select Southern Ontario destination events considered subject to applicable travel fees.
- Luxe Event Co. carries $5 million in liability insurance.

Capacity is not presented as a fixed servings-per-hour promise. Dessert selection, service duration, staffing, venue access, and guest flow remain event-specific.

## Deferred Operational Boundary

The intake does not define:

- Every possible multi-dessert combination as a standard inclusion.
- One universal cart footprint.
- One universal electrical, water, or weather configuration.
- One fixed staffing model or servings-per-hour rate.

The page therefore states that:

- Multiple dessert types can be requested, with the available pairing, equipment, staffing, and service format confirmed in the event proposal.
- Indoor or outdoor conditions, footprint, access, utilities, weather, and preparation requirements are reviewed for the selected experience.
- No unrestricted combination or universal venue specification should be assumed.

Final production specifications should replace this boundary only after client approval.
