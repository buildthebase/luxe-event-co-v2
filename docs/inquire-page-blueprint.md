# Inquire / Contact Page Blueprint

## Governing Sources

This page follows the Luxe Event Co. master specification, the completed client
intake, the approved Phase 1 inquiry boundary, and the Phase 2 visual system.

## Purpose and Conversion

The page prepares qualified visitors to begin an inquiry for one or several
Luxe experiences. Its primary CTA is **Begin Your Inquiry**.

The website owns explanation, preparation, public contact access, the handoff
CTA, and permitted click measurement. The selected third-party platform owns
the operational form, conditional questions, submission storage, confirmation,
proposal, agreement, deposit, payment, reminders, CRM, and follow-up.

Until the production third-party URL is approved in `siteConfig.inquiry.url`,
the primary CTA uses the approved public email as a transparent fallback. The
page does not embed or invent a form destination.

## Required Information

The visible event brief covers date, location, estimated guest count, event
type, service duration, indoor or outdoor setting, interested experience or
experiences, branding requirements, and special requests.

## Structured Data

The page publishes `ContactPage`, `WebPage`, and `BreadcrumbList`. The primary
Luxe Event Co. `Organization` entity contains the approved public email,
telephone, and inquiry `ContactPoint`.

No `Offer`, transaction, third-party platform, or unsupported physical-address
schema is added.
