# Weddings Page Blueprint

Status: Implemented locally for review. Not approved for commit or production release.

## Governing Sources

This page is governed by:

- The Luxe Event Co. master specification.
- The complete client-submitted Luxe Event Co. intake.
- The approved Phase 1 page contract, search intent, internal-linking, entity, conversion, and content-differentiation plans.
- The approved Phase 2 visual system, signature elements, Home, Experiences Hub, experience pages, and Events Hub.

## Purpose And Conversion

The Weddings page establishes Luxe as a credible wedding vendor while encouraging cohesive multi-service bookings. The primary CTA is **Plan Your Wedding Experience**.

The page is organized around the wedding timeline rather than around three generic service categories. Each division has a distinct role, and all three remain directly linked.

## Content Architecture

1. Wedding-specific hero and trust proof.
2. Overview of wedding applications and independent or combined booking.
3. Pre-ceremony, cocktail-hour, reception, dessert, late-night, and morning-after possibilities.
4. Distinct Coffee Bar, Sweet Cart, and Seating Rentals roles.
5. Suggested wedding combinations that are explicitly not fixed packages.
6. Custom drinks, menus, cups, signage, cart branding, and presentation.
7. Planner, coordinator, venue, vendor, setup, and logistics considerations.
8. Permission-gated wedding gallery placeholders.
9. Permission-gated wedding testimonial placement.
10. Visible wedding planning questions and factual answers.
11. Wedding-specific final inquiry panel.

## Confirmed Business Facts

- Coffee Bar and Sweet Cart setup and takedown are included.
- Rental delivery, setup, teardown, pickup, quantities, and fees require proposal confirmation.
- Coffee, dessert, and rentals can be coordinated through one inquiry and proposal journey.
- Custom beverages, menus, cups, signage, and cart presentation are available subject to final direction and production requirements.
- Luxe works with planners, coordinators, venues, and relevant vendor teams.
- A 30% non-refundable retainer and signed contract secure the booking.
- The remaining balance is due seven days before the event.
- Luxe recommends early booking for weddings and peak-season dates without publishing an unsupported universal lead-time promise.
- Luxe serves Toronto and the GTA and considers select Southern Ontario destination weddings with applicable travel fees.
- Luxe carries $5 million liability insurance.

## Deferred Or Permission-Gated Content

- Exact universal lead time.
- Universal venue footprint, power, water, outdoor, or weather requirements.
- Universal rental logistics and fees.
- Named planners, venues, couples, or vendor partnerships.
- Wedding testimonials, ratings, and quotations.
- Final wedding photography and video.

## Search And AEO

- Primary search intent: **Wedding coffee bar Toronto**.
- Supporting topics: wedding dessert cart, wedding rentals, cocktail-hour coffee, customized wedding beverages, and coordinated wedding experiences.
- Visible content answers every question required by the master specification.
- Direct links connect Coffee Bar, Sweet Cart, Seating Rentals, Events, Gallery, FAQ, and Inquire.
- The page does not make unsupported guarantees or publish a city-keyword list.

## Schema

The page uses:

- `Service`.
- `WebPage`.
- `BreadcrumbList`.

It does not use `Event` structured data because the page describes wedding services rather than one public, date-specific, bookable event. It also does not use `Product`, `Offer`, `Review`, `AggregateRating`, or fabricated FAQ markup.

## Responsive Behaviour

- Large desktop uses an asymmetric wedding hero, a horizontal day sequence, and three distinct experience expressions.
- Mid-width layouts recompose the hero, experience roles, section headers, and coordination content before columns become compressed.
- Mobile uses one clear timeline and one experience at a time with full-width CTAs and no horizontal overflow.
- Full-bleed contrast chapters retain the shared page-width contract and approved grid background.
