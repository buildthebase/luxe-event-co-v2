# Phase 4B.1: Priority ICP research profiles

## Decision

The five ICPs are research lenses, not five new page types and not five large
sections to add throughout the website. Their detailed question registry and
operational dependencies are maintained in `app/aeo-audience-research.ts`.

This extends the earlier four-segment audience model in one important way:
Industry Partners now have a separate research profile. Planners, venues, and
other vendors can overlap with a client audience while still asking a distinct
set of coordination, responsibility, and professional-risk questions.

## Profile summary

| Profile | Core decision | Informational concerns | Operational concerns | Commercial concerns | Trust concerns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wedding Clients | Whether the experiences belong in the wedding flow and can be coordinated with the vendor team | Moment, format, combinations, menu, presentation, dietary needs | Capacity, service timing, staffing, footprint, utilities, setup, weather, venue schedule | Price variables, minimums, travel, booking timing, retainer | Comparable proof, coordination quality, insurance |
| Corporate Clients | Whether Luxe can meet the brief and internal approval process with low coordination risk | Use cases, professional fit, service combinations | Throughput, staffing, access, simultaneous sites, multiple dates, venue rules | Quote variables, procurement, invoicing and approvals | Corporate proof, insurance, contingency planning, clear ownership |
| Brand and Agency Clients | Whether Luxe can translate a campaign brief into a feasible brand interaction | Branding surfaces, activation format, campaign role | Production workflow, throughput, utilities, artwork approvals, installation | Customization depth, quantities, locations, dates and approval effects | Brand accuracy, confidentiality, agency workflow, production and venue compliance |
| Private-Event Clients | Which experience fits the occasion without requiring professional planning knowledge | Occasion fit, combinations, personalization | Home or venue fit, access, utilities, outdoor conditions, host preparation | Inclusions, price variables, minimums, travel, value of combining services | Comparable proof, clear guidance, venue care, confirmed setup responsibilities |
| Industry Partners | Whether Luxe will integrate cleanly into the wider event plan and respect role boundaries | Event flow, room plan, responsibilities and handoffs | Technical requirements, loading, setup, service, strike and event-day communication | Scope, revisions, travel, rentals, overtime, referrals and promise boundaries | Insurance, accurate technical information, partner protection, proper credit |

## Placement and anti-bloat rules

- Wedding questions primarily belong on Weddings; corporate buyer questions on
  Corporate Events; campaign questions on Brand Activations; occasion-selection
  questions on the relevant private-event page.
- Service mechanics belong on the corresponding experience page.
- A shared policy should be answered once in FAQ and linked contextually rather
  than rewritten on every event page.
- Industry Partners remain a cross-page decision lens. No partner landing page
  is justified by this step.
- Later research should prefer compact answers, comparisons, checklists, and
  contextual links. A question does not automatically require a new section.
- No public copy was added in Step 4B.1.

## Publication dependencies

The research registry records all required Phase 4B operational facts as
confirmed, partially confirmed, or unverified. Partially confirmed topics may
use only the approved portion of the fact. Unverified details remain content
dependencies and cannot be inferred from competitors or industry norms.

The dependencies cover guest capacity, hourly throughput, staffing, setup and
teardown timing, footprint, power, water, outdoor limits, travel, minimums,
pricing variables, rental inventory, simultaneous setups, multi-day capability,
branding lead times, insurance, and venue coordination.
