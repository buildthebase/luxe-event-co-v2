## Local-page gate

No city page was created. A future location page requires:

- unique local event evidence;
- approved local photography;
- first-hand venue and logistics knowledge;
- permissioned local testimonials or case studies;
- a distinct and useful local search need; and
- enough original operational information to avoid a city-name template.
