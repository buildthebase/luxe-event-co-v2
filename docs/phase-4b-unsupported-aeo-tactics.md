# Phase 4B.22 — Unsupported AEO Tactics

Date: July 27, 2026  
Status: Guardrails documented and implementation audited

## Outcome

The current Phase 4B implementation does not depend on generic AI keywords,
machine-only content, route multiplication, thin city pages, exact-match heading
stuffing, universal FAQ formatting, hidden priority answers, excessive schema,
unsupported statistics, fabricated proof, fake mentions, or AI-oriented
rewriting.

No public content or route was added for this step. The useful implementation is
the enforceable guardrail: a query or research finding does not automatically
create a page, heading, FAQ entry, schema type, statistic, or proof claim.

## `llms.txt` decision

The existing `public/llms.txt` file is retained as an optional, isolated summary
of already-public facts. It is not required for launch and carries no ranking,
indexing, citation, or AI-visibility promise.

No website behaviour depends on it:

- it is not a permanent page in the route map;
- it is not linked from navigation;
- it is not included in the XML sitemap;
- it is not referenced by structured data or canonical tags;
- it is not required by the robots policy;
- it does not contain a unique answer unavailable on the visible website; and
- it cannot substitute for crawlable pages, accurate content, internal links,
  or first-party evidence.

Removing the file solely to demonstrate that it is optional would not improve
the website. If retained, it must remain a concise factual derivative of visible
approved content.

## Route and content controls

- The architecture remains at 16 permanent routes.
- No city-specific route exists.
- Long-tail and measurement findings return to the definitive existing page
  before any new route is considered.
- Question headings are used where a real question is answered, not as a global
  heading pattern.
- Comparisons, processes, inclusions, timelines, logistics summaries, proof,
  captions, and visible direct answers remain distinct formats.
- Priority pricing and planning answers remain visible before secondary
  disclosures.

## Schema and evidence controls

- Schema remains limited to visible, accurate, justified page and entity types.
- Event-service pages do not use `Event` schema.
- No self-serving rating or review markup is used.
- FAQ markup is derived from matching visible questions and answers.
- No hidden or incomplete content is marked up.
- Confirmed statistics retain their scope; unverified throughput, staffing,
  timing, utilities, wait times, and outcomes remain unpublished.
- Photography, testimonials, venues, campaigns, outcomes, and case studies stay
  gated until their facts and permissions are approved.
- Approved organization-name use never implies endorsement or testimonial.

## Writing control

Public answers are written for couples, planners, coordinators, venues,
corporate teams, agencies, and private-event hosts. The answer comes first,
followed by decision factors, the relevant Luxe process, and a useful next step.
Copy must not address algorithms, AI systems, keywords, extraction, or ranking.

## Deliberate deviations

- `llms.txt` was retained because the requirement is that it remain optional,
  not that it must be absent.
- No public warning or AEO-policy section was added to the website.
- No new schema, FAQ item, city page, resource page, or analytics event was
  introduced.
- This is the Step 4B.22 guardrail audit, not the final Phase 4B hard pass.
