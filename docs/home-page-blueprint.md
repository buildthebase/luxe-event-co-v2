# Complete Home Page Blueprint

Status: Implemented locally for review. Not approved for commit or production release.

## Page Purpose

Introduce Luxe Event Co. as the parent organization, establish credible operational trust, explain the relationship between all three divisions, and direct visitors into the correct service-led or event-led journey.

- Primary: **Plan Your Event** → `/inquire`

## Content and Evidence Rules

- The parent brand and all three division names appear in visible copy.
- Weddings, corporate events, brand activations, and private events receive direct contextual links.
- The Home page links directly to every critical page required by the Phase 1 internal-link map.
- The `$5 million liability insurance`, coffee capacity, and simultaneous setup claims come from approved project evidence.
- No package, price, response, availability, or event-day outcome is invented.
- The current inquiry route remains an explanatory handoff until the third-party platform is approved.

## Search and AEO Coverage

- Page title targets the strategic Home intent: luxury event experiences in Toronto.
- Visible copy identifies Luxe Event Co., all three divisions, Toronto, the GTA, and Southern Ontario.
- Visible event copy covers weddings, corporate events, brand activations, and private events without keyword repetition.
- Important facts are server-rendered and available without interaction.
- Internal links use descriptive destination language.
- No hidden SEO copy or unsupported schema claim is used.

## Structured Data

The global graph now represents:

- `Organization` for Luxe Event Co.
- Three connected division `Organization` entities using `department` and `parentOrganization`.
- Three `Service` entities provided by Luxe Event Co. and branded by the relevant division entity.

`LocalBusiness` remains intentionally deferred because a publishable address and confirmed business-profile model have not been supplied. This follows the master specification requirement to use it only where accurate.

## Responsive Behaviour

- Desktop uses a controlled hero split, editorial section transitions, connected horizontal systems, and full-width visual moments.
- Tablet rebalances multi-column sections without reducing text below readable sizes.
- Mobile recomposes the hero, selector, proof, event links, working principles, testimonials, and service-area content into purpose-built vertical sequences.
- No required content depends on hover or animation.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable reveal and transform durations through the global safeguard.

## Site-Wide Pattern Contribution

The reviewed Home page establishes patterns that future complete-page blueprints generally inherit:

- Fine ivory grid as the connective canvas.
- Taupe narrative fields, softer taupe transitions, and matte-black emphasis chapters.
- Full-bleed surfaces with content constrained to the shared editorial grid.
- Matte-black emphasis for verified operational proof such as the `$5M` liability-insurance fact.
- Approved typography, tracking, spacing, rules, focus states, responsive recomposition, and reduced-motion behaviour.
- Contextual inquiry panels, descriptive internal links, and signature modules where required.

Future pages must not copy the Home section order, hero sequence, proof placement, or CTA language. Their surface sequence and reusable-module placement are defined by page purpose, search intent, audience concerns, evidence, and the relevant complete-page blueprint.
