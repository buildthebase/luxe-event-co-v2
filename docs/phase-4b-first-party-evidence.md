# Phase 4B.15 — First-Party Evidence

Date: July 27, 2026  
Status: Confirmed evidence paired; unavailable proof retained as dependencies

## Evidence added or reinforced

The website already contained several forms of approved first-party proof. This
step makes their status and placement explicit:

- Coffee Bar pairs its answers with confirmed inclusions, named beverage
  selections, capacity up to 500 guests, up to three simultaneous setups, and
  confirmed setup/takedown inclusion.
- Sweet Cart pairs its answers with confirmed inclusions, named desserts,
  sauces and toppings, capacity up to 400 guests, up to three simultaneous
  setups, and confirmed setup/takedown inclusion.
- Seating Rentals pairs its answers with six confirmed rental categories and
  clearly identifies delivery, setup, teardown, inventory, and logistics details
  that must be defined in the proposal or an approved policy.
- Corporate pages use the five organization names and logo files approved for
  display: OPTrust, CST Savings, Convergint, ICNA Canada, and Waste Connections
  of Canada.
- Booking answers use the approved signed-contract requirement, 30%
  non-refundable retainer, and remaining-balance deadline of seven days before
  the event.
- Local answers use the approved Toronto and GTA service area, qualified
  Southern Ontario availability, and travel-fee boundary.
- Insurance references use the confirmed $5 million liability-insurance amount.

Visible section labels now distinguish confirmed inclusions, capacity, setup
scope, rental categories, and operating facts from general marketing copy.
Evidence-bearing sections also carry audit attributes so the content source,
qualification boundary, and future asset state can be checked without adding
more visible prose.

## Evidence not yet publishable

The following remain dependencies:

- original event photography;
- real completed-event examples;
- customization photography;
- branded cup, menu, signage, or cart examples;
- genuine testimonials;
- wedding gallery evidence;
- corporate gallery or case-study evidence; and
- documented multi-service event examples.

These items require the actual file or event record, permission, accurate
context, and approved attribution. Organization-name permission does not imply
permission to publish a campaign, event photograph, testimonial, result, or
case study.

## Menu qualification

Confirmed beverage, dessert, sauce, and topping selections remain visible
because they are useful first-party service evidence. The remaining menu
dependency has been narrowed to final availability, dietary notes, effective
date, and internal version ownership. Until those details are supplied, the
site does not present every selection as permanently available for every event.

## Deliberate deviations

- No stock, generated, or representative photography was used as proof of Luxe
  work.
- No planning combination was relabelled as an actual completed event.
- No quotation, venue, campaign detail, or operational outcome was invented.
- No approved organization was presented as an endorsement or testimonial.
- No new case-study, gallery, testimonial, or resource route was created.

## Publication gate for future proof

An image or event example becomes publishable only when Luxe provides:

1. the original file or accurate event record;
2. ownership and written publication permission;
3. the relevant service, event type, location, and operational context;
4. approved client, venue, or testimonial attribution where applicable;
5. factual alt text and a meaningful caption; and
6. a stable public filename and appropriate responsive derivative.
