## Purpose And Conversion

The Brand Activations page speaks directly to agencies, marketers, experiential teams, retail brands, public relations teams, and campaign or content teams. The primary CTA is **Create a Branded Experience**.

The page uses a campaign-canvas visual language rather than the Corporate Events page’s operational-system composition. The experience is presented as a tangible brand interaction, not a generic cart with a logo applied.

## Content Architecture

1. Campaign-led activation hero with agency, scale, and insurance proof.
2. Explanation of a branded hospitality experience.
3. Six campaign surfaces: cups, signage, cart branding, drink names, colour alignment, and tailored menus.
4. Product launches, retail activations, grand openings, trade shows, conferences, and campaign events.
5. Distinct Coffee Bar, Sweet Cart, and Seating Rentals or styling roles.
6. Multiple setups, multi-day work, and carefully qualified multiple-location planning.
7. Content and photography opportunities with coordination and permission requirements.
8. Approved organization proof.
9. Permission-gated activation gallery and case-study placeholders.
10. Campaign brief, brand assets, operations, approvals, and production planning requirements.
11. Visible activation-specific FAQs.
12. Activation-specific final inquiry panel.

## Qualified Or Deferred Claims

- Multiple-location execution is not presented as an unconditional confirmed capability. It is reviewed and confirmed only after Luxe assesses the locations, dates, travel, staffing, equipment, production requirements, access, and handoffs.
- No universal activation lead time is published. Custom production, quantities, approvals, vendors, campaign complexity, and event dates determine the planning timeline.
- The recommended client asset list is framed as planning readiness. Exact file formats, print specifications, quantities, and production deadlines remain confirmation-dependent.
- Named-client approval does not imply permission to publish campaign details, outcomes, testimonials, quotations, imagery, or case studies.

## Schema

The page uses:

- `Service`.
- `WebPage`.
- `BreadcrumbList`.

It does not use `Event`, `Product`, `Offer`, `Review`, `AggregateRating`, or fabricated FAQ markup.

## Responsive Behaviour

- Large desktop uses a two-column campaign-canvas hero, six-part brand surface, three service media, and a horizontal production pathway.
- Tablet layouts recompose service and scale chapters before columns become compressed.
- Mobile presents each campaign surface and planning requirement sequentially, maintains the shared two-line H1 contract, and retains full-width CTAs.
- Contrast chapters and the site-wide fine grid remain consistent with the approved visual system.
