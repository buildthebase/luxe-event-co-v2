## Anti-bloat decisions

- No dedicated comparison route, article, or generic comparison table was
  created.
- Dessert cart versus table and hosted versus self-serve share one complete
  answer because the underlying service-model decision is the same.
- Dessert-choice guidance was added to the existing three dessert cards rather
  than repeated in another FAQ.
- Existing format, booking, and setup answers were expanded before new FAQs
  were considered.
- Only three new FAQs were necessary: traditional coffee catering, venue coffee
  service, and standard corporate catering.

## Neutrality and accuracy controls

- Every alternative is described as appropriate in a suitable context.
- Comparisons use operational decision factors rather than claims of universal
  superiority.
- Venue service, traditional catering, self-service, separate specialists, and
  delivery-only rentals are not characterized as lower quality.
- No competitor, price, speed, staffing, footprint, utility, or return claim was
  invented.
- Each answer connects naturally to its definitive Luxe service page.
