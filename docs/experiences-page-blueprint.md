# Experiences Hub Blueprint

## Governing Sources

This blueprint is governed by:

- The Luxe Event Co. master specification.
- The latest client-submitted Luxe Brands intake form.
- The approved Phase 1 architecture, search intent, internal-linking, entity, and conversion plans.
- The approved Phase 2 visual system and recognizable signature elements.

When sources differ, verified client intake facts take precedence over assumptions. Missing operational facts remain deferred rather than invented.

## Page Contract

- Route: `/experiences`
- Page type: indexable Experiences Hub
- Purpose: present Luxe Coffee Bar, Luxe Sweet Cart, and Luxe Seating Rentals as distinct but complementary experiences.
- Primary CTA: Explore an Experience.
- Secondary CTA: Plan Your Event.
- Primary search intent: event experiences Toronto.
- Supporting topics: coffee bar, dessert cart, event rentals, independent bookings, and combined Luxe experiences.

## Content Sequence

1. Parent-brand introduction and primary journey.
2. Prominent Coffee, Dessert, and Seating selector.
3. Direct answers about independent and combined booking.
4. Three differentiated editorial experience features.
5. Comparison by event need and desired atmosphere.
6. Curated combined-experience examples.
7. Links to every major event type.
8. Gallery preview with explicit approved-asset placeholders.
9. Explanatory planning pathway.
10. Contextual Experiences inquiry panel.

The sequence moves from recognition to comparison, then from event context to inquiry. It does not reproduce the complete inclusions, customization, gallery, FAQ, or conversion content reserved for each division landing page.

## Division Differentiation

### Luxe Coffee Bar

- Role: crafted hospitality and a refined cafe atmosphere.
- Operational proof used: Cafe Cart and Signature Coffee Bar formats, professional barista service, hot and iced espresso beverages, signature drinks, premium non-coffee options, and a confirmed capacity of up to 500 guests.
- Visual language: dark, warm, circular, and ritual-led.

### Luxe Sweet Cart

- Role: live, made-to-order dessert preparation and guest interaction.
- Operational proof used: mini Dutch pancakes, Belgian waffles on a stick, mini donuts, sauces and toppings, signage and styling, and a confirmed capacity of up to 400 guests.
- Visual language: soft, tactile, playful, and elevated.

### Luxe Seating Rentals

- Role: define how guests arrive, gather, dine, and move through the room.
- Operational proof used: chairs, tables, cocktail tables, tents, linens, lighting, and event setup considerations.
- Visual language: architectural, structured, and room-led.
- No unsupported capacity claim is made.

## Booking Clarity

The page answers the two required AEO questions in visible copy:

- Each division can be booked independently.
- Multiple Luxe experiences can be combined.

Coffee and dessert are identified as a frequently combined pairing from the client intake. Coffee, dessert, and rentals may also be considered in one coordinated event inquiry. Curated combinations are planning directions, not fixed packages.

## Search, AEO, And Schema

The title and description target the approved Experiences Hub intent without competing with division-specific targets.

Visible content:

- Names Luxe Event Co. and all three divisions.
- Establishes Toronto and GTA context.
- Defines how the divisions relate.
- Explains independent and combined booking.
- Provides concise, fact-based differentiators.

Structured data:

- `CollectionPage`
- `WebPage`
- `ItemList`
- `BreadcrumbList`

The `ItemList` mirrors the three visible experiences and links to their permanent URLs.

## Visual And Responsive Direction

- The page uses the approved fine grid, colour system, typography, contrast rhythm, and restrained motion rules.
- Distinction comes from composition and visual language, not separate disconnected design systems.
- Editorial features replace symmetrical service cards.
- Dark sections carry booking clarity and comparison.
- Taupe and soft surfaces create separation without mechanical striping.
- Mobile recomposes every grid into a clear reading order and keeps the three experiences individually recognizable.

## Asset Boundary

The gallery preview intentionally uses branded placeholders marked `awaiting-approved-photography`. Before production photography replaces them, approved assets are required for:

- Coffee service in use.
- Dessert preparation in use.
- A completed seating or rental setting.

Every final image must be associated with the correct experience, event context, caption, alt text, and approved usage rights.

## Acceptance Notes

- All required blueprint sections are represented.
- The primary CTA is unambiguous.
- Every experience and major event page is linked.
- Independent and combined booking are answered directly.
- Seating capacity is not invented.
- Detail-page content is not duplicated unnecessarily.
