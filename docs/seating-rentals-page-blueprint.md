## Experience Differentiation

Luxe Seating Rentals is defined by:

- Layout before catalogue.
- Comfort, sightlines, circulation, and service zones.
- The relationship between inventory and the room around it.
- Indoor and outdoor planning considerations.
- Furniture and structure as part of the event experience rather than isolated products.

This is distinct from Luxe Coffee Bar's crafted beverage hospitality and Luxe Sweet Cart's live dessert preparation. The page connects those divisions only where the room materially supports service placement, guest flow, and gathering.

## Deferred Operational Boundary

The intake does not yet confirm:

- Accurate inventory quantities.
- A universal delivery area or delivery-fee schedule.
- Whether delivery is available for every order or location.
- Whether setup is universally included.
- Whether teardown or pickup is universally included.
- Load-in, access, after-hours, or timing rules.
- Damage, cancellation, replacement, or rental-use policies.
- Tent engineering, permitting, surface, weather, or wind requirements.
- Fixed product or package pricing.

The page therefore states that:

- Delivery availability, timing, access, and fees must be confirmed in the proposal.
- Setup scope must be confirmed by inventory, quantity, floor plan, access, and schedule.
- Teardown and pickup responsibilities must be confirmed in the proposal.
- Outdoor suitability requires an event-specific review.
- Final inventory and policy copy depend on client approval.

These statements are an accuracy boundary, not final operational policy. They should be replaced only after Luxe supplies and approves the relevant information.

## Schema

The page implements:

- `Service`
- `WebPage`
- `BreadcrumbList`

The Service provider references the parent Luxe Event Co. Organization entity.

The page intentionally does not implement:

- `Product`
- `Offer`
- `AggregateOffer`

Those types must not be introduced unless fixed, publicly visible products or packages meet the applicable structured-data requirements.

The visible FAQ content is not represented with `FAQPage` in this blueprint because the requested page schema contract specifies Service, BreadcrumbList, and WebPage only.
